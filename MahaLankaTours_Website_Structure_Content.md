## 4. Key Destinations Highlighted

1. **Sigiriya (Ancient Fortress)**: 5th-century palace in the sky with ancient frescoes and water gardens.
2. **Ella (Hill Country)**: Cloud-shrouded mountain village with lush tea estates and iconic arches.
3. **Mirissa (Coastal Gem)**: Golden sands, palm trees, vibrant nightlife, and blue whale watching.
4. **Kandy (Cultural Capital)**: Nestled among hills surrounding a serene lake, home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.
5. **Yala National Park**: Highest density of Sri Lankan leopards in the world along with wild elephants and sloth bears.
6. **Galle Fort**: UNESCO World Heritage Dutch colonial ramparts on the Indian Ocean.

## 5. Interactive Features Specs

- **Hero Search Widget**: Search by location, category, date, and traveler count.
- **Tour Category Tabs**: Filter packages instantly without page reload.
- **Itinerary Cost Estimator**: Users select trip length, accommodation standard, and group size to receive an instant live price estimate.
- **Quick-View Modal**: View day-by-day itineraries with photos, inclusions, and instant booking modal.
- **PHP Form Handlers**: Server-side processing for bookings (`api/book_tour.php`) and contact submissions (`api/contact_submit.php`).
